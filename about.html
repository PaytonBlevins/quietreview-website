About Us

About QuietReview

QuietReview was built by a team of professionals with years of hands-on solar industry experience. The kind of experience that shows exactly where proposals tend to get confusing, and exactly which clauses are worth a second look.

We started QuietReview because most homeowners only get one perspective on a solar deal, and that's the one from the company trying to sell it to them. We wanted to offer another option, an independent set of eyes, paid for by the homeowner, with no stake in whether you sign. We also understand that solar is a very confusing industry as it is ever-changing and filled with hundreds of options. We're here to take some of that load off of you.

How It Works

How we use AI.

Every review follows the same two steps — on every tier, no exceptions.

Step 1 — AI Review

Reads fast, checks the same categories every time, and doesn't lose consistency by the hundredth document the way a person might.

Step 2 — Human Review

A person reviews the AI's findings against your actual document before anything is sent to you.

Either way, the same independent standard applies: no financial relationship with any solar company, no incentive to tell you what you want to hear.

What QuietReview is not.

  • Not a law firm — we don't provide legal advice or legal representation
  • Not financial advisors — we don't provide financial or investment advice
  • Not affiliated with any solar company, installer, or lender, and we never accept payment or referral fees from them
  • Not a source of legal opinions, financial plans, or recommendations to sign or walk away

Any decision with legal or financial consequences should involve a licensed professional where appropriate.
