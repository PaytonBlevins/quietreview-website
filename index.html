Questions

Before you ask, we probably answered it.

What exactly does QuietReview analyze?+

We review the actual proposal, contract, or bid you upload — pricing, payment terms, financing structure, equipment, and warranties, plus any clauses worth understanding before you sign. We tell you what's typical, what's unusual, and what's worth asking about, all in plain language.

Is this legal advice?+

No. QuietReview is not a law firm, and nothing we provide is legal advice. We help you understand what a document says — we don't represent you, and we're not a substitute for a licensed attorney if you need one.

What format does my contract need to be in?+

Any format works. Upload a PDF, a photo, or a scan — whatever you have is fine.

How do I get my report?+

Select a tier, upload your document, and check out. We email your finished report to the address you provide at checkout.

What if I only have a photo of my contract?+

That's completely fine. A clear photo of every page is all we need — just make sure the text is readable.

How long does it take to receive my report?+

Snapshot Review and Deeper Analysis reports are delivered within 24 hours. Confidence Walkthrough orders, which include a personal video, are delivered within 24–48 hours.

Does AI review my document?+

Yes. Every report starts with AI analysis, then is reviewed by a person before it's sent to you — that's true across every tier, Snapshot Review included. AI doesn't change who we work for.

Do you work with any solar companies?+

No. QuietReview doesn't accept payment, referrals, or compensation from any solar company, installer, or lender. Our only client is you.

What makes QuietReview independent?+

We're paid directly by the homeowner, not by a company trying to sell you a system. That means our only incentive is to give you an accurate, honest read of what you're looking at.

Can I get a refund if I'm not satisfied?+

If we make an error in your report, or don't deliver within the promised window, contact us and we'll make it right — including a refund if appropriate. Because each report involves real analysis time on our end, we're not able to offer refunds based simply on what the report finds.

What happens after I pay?+

You'll get an immediate email confirming your order and delivery timeframe. Our team reviews your document and prepares your report, then emails it directly to you — no further steps required on your end.
