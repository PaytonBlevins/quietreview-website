Terms & Disclaimer

QuietReview helps you understand a proposal in plain language. It does not replace a lawyer, a financial advisor, or your own judgment.

QuietReview is not a law firm, and nothing we provide is legal advice or legal representation. We are not financial advisors, and nothing we provide is financial or investment advice. Our reports do not constitute a recommendation to accept, reject, or sign any agreement — that decision, and any decision with legal or financial consequences, should involve a licensed professional where appropriate.

Independence

Who pays us, and who doesn't.

QuietReview is client-paid, not commission-paid. We have no financial relationship with any solar company, installer, or lender named in a proposal we review, and we never accept payment or referral fees from them. The only person we work for is the one who paid for the report.

Methodology

How reports are prepared.

Every review follows the same two steps, on every tier, no exceptions.

Step 1 — AI Review

Reads fast, checks the same categories every time, and doesn't lose consistency by the hundredth document the way a person might.

Step 2 — Human Review

A person reviews the AI's findings against your actual document before anything is sent to you.

Reports reflect the documents you provide. If a document is incomplete, illegible, or missing a page, our ability to identify every relevant term is limited accordingly — and we'll say so in the report rather than guess.

Delivery & Refunds

What to expect after you order.

Snapshot Review and Deeper Analysis reports are delivered within 24 hours. Confidence Walkthrough orders, which include a personal video, are delivered within 24–48 hours.

If we make an error in your report, or don't deliver within the promised window, contact us and we'll make it right — including a refund if appropriate. Because each report involves real analysis time on our end, we're not able to offer refunds based simply on what the report finds.

Your Documents

What happens to what you upload.

  • Documents you upload are used only to prepare your report.
  • We do not share your documents with any solar company, installer, or lender.
  • We do not sell or share your documents or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.
  • Documents are retained only as long as reasonably needed to deliver your report and respond to follow-up questions.
