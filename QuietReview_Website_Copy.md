## 2. FAQ

**1. What exactly does QuietReview analyze?**
We review the actual proposal, contract, or bid you upload — pricing, payment terms, financing structure, equipment, and warranties, plus any clauses worth understanding before you sign. We tell you what's typical, what's unusual, and what's worth asking about, all in plain language.

**2. Is this legal advice?**
No. QuietReview is not a law firm, and nothing we provide is legal advice. We help you understand what a document says — we don't represent you, and we're not a substitute for a licensed attorney if you need one.

**3. What format does my contract need to be in?**
Any format works. Upload a PDF, a photo, or a scan — whatever you have is fine.

**4. How do I get my report?**
Select a tier, upload your document, and check out. We email your finished report to the address you provide at checkout.

**5. What if I only have a photo of my contract?**
That's completely fine. A clear photo of every page is all we need — just make sure the text is readable.

**6. How long does it take to receive my report?**
Snapshot Review and Deeper Analysis reports are delivered within 24 hours. Confidence Walkthrough orders, which include a personal video, are delivered within 24–48 hours.

**7. Does AI review my document?**
Yes. Every report starts with AI analysis. Snapshot Reviews are generated by AI and checked against our internal quality standards before they're sent to you. Deeper Analysis and Confidence Walkthrough reports also get reviewed by a person before delivery. Either way, the same independent standard applies — AI doesn't change who we work for.

**8. Do you work with any solar companies?**
No. QuietReview doesn't accept payment, referrals, or compensation from any solar company, installer, or lender. Our only client is you.

**9. What makes QuietReview independent?**
We're paid directly by the homeowner, not by a company trying to sell you a system. That means our only incentive is to give you an accurate, honest read of what you're looking at.

**10. Can I get a refund if I'm not satisfied?**
If we make an error in your report, or don't deliver within the promised window, contact us and we'll make it right — including a refund if appropriate. Because each report involves real analysis time on our end, we're not able to offer refunds based simply on what the report finds.

You'll get an immediate email confirming your order and delivery timeframe. Our team reviews your document and prepares your report, then emails it directly to you — no further steps required on your end.

## 3. About Page

QuietReview was built by a team of professionals with years of hands-on solar industry experience. The kind of experience that shows exactly where proposals tend to get confusing, and exactly which clauses are worth a second look.

We started QuietReview because most homeowners only get one perspective on a solar deal, and that's the one from the company trying to sell it to them. We wanted to offer another option, an independent set of eyes, paid for by the homeowner, with no stake in whether you sign. We also understand that solar is a very confusing industry as it is ever-changing and filled with hundreds of options. We're here to take some of that load off of you.

**How We Use AI**
Every review starts with AI. It reads fast, checks the same categories every time, and doesn't lose consistency by the hundredth document the way a person might. Snapshot Reviews are generated by AI and checked against our internal quality standards before they're sent to you. Deeper Analysis and Confidence Walkthrough reports go a step further — a person reviews the AI's findings against the actual document before anything reaches you. Either way, the same independent standard applies: no financial relationship with any solar company, no incentive to tell you what you want to hear.

**What QuietReview is not**
QuietReview is not a law firm, and we don't provide legal advice or legal representation. We're not financial advisors, and we don't provide financial or investment advice. We're not affiliated with any solar company, installer, or lender, and we never accept payment or referral fees from them. What we offer is a plain-language, independent read of your proposal — not a legal opinion, not a financial plan, and not a recommendation to sign or walk away. Any decision with legal or financial consequences should involve a licensed professional where appropriate.
